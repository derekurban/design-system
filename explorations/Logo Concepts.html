Logo concepts Resolved, shipped

Question
A clean, minimal mark about chaos turning into order, or technology and people.
Explored
Eight rounds, newest at the top. 1–3: 36 open concepts. 4: variations on bracket and squiggle. 5: bracket types (square chosen). 6–8: squiggle types; round 7 also tried 12 hover animations.
Outcome

8c Looser became the Default logo: a loose scribble between square brackets, the closing bracket in green. Files are assets/logos/logo-on-light.svg, logo-on-dark.svg and logo-mono.svg. It has a “Default logo” card in the Brand section and a “The logo” section in the readme.

The animation is Draw: the left bracket fades in, the scribble draws, then the green bracket closes from the right. The other round 7 animations were not used.
